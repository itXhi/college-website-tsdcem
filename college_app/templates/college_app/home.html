Thakur Shree DPS College of Engineering & Management (TSDCEM)
AICTE Approved MU Affiliated
2025–26 Launch Year

Thakur Shree DPS College of Engineering & Management (TSDCEM) is a newly established engineering college located in Gokhiware, Vasai (East), Palghar, Maharashtra. It is an initiative of the well-known Thakur Educational Group, which already runs several reputed institutions in Mumbai and has earned a strong reputation for academic excellence, discipline, and student-focused education.

The college is set to begin its academic journey from the 2025–26 academic year, with a clear aim of providing modern technical and management education that prepares students for real-world challenges. The institution is being built with the vision of becoming a leading center for innovation, practical learning, and future-ready careers in the region.

TSDCEM is designed to create a strong academic environment where students can develop both technical competence and personal confidence. The campus experience is intended to support intellectual growth, professional skills, and holistic development through a balanced mix of academics, projects, and co-curricular activities.

TSDCEM is committed to maintaining strong academic credibility through recognized approvals and affiliations. The college is approved by the AICTE, affiliated with the University of Mumbai, and recognized by the Government of Maharashtra, ensuring that its programs meet national and state educational standards.

These affiliations and approvals are important because they give students confidence that their degrees will hold academic value, be recognized nationally, and support future higher studies, competitive exams, and career opportunities. The institution is focused on building a strong foundation that aligns with the expectations of industry, regulators, and society.

  • Approved by AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education)

  • Affiliated with University of Mumbai

  • Recognized by Government of Maharashtra

Currently, TSDCEM mainly offers undergraduate engineering programs (B.E./B.Tech) in multiple streams such as Computer Engineering(CE), Information Technology(IT), Artificial Intelligence and Data Science(AIDS), Electronics and Computer science(ECS) and other emerging engineering branches like masters in computer application(MCA) that are aligned with modern industry needs. These programs are designed to provide students with a strong base in core technical concepts along with practical exposure.

The academic structure is expected to encourage students to think critically, solve real-world problems, and develop the confidence needed to succeed in both higher studies and professional life. The curriculum is likely to blend foundational theory, laboratory learning, project work, and career-oriented skill development.

  • Computer Engineering(CE)

  • Information Technology(IT)

  • Artificial Intelligence and Data Science(AIDS)

  • Electronics and Computer Science(ECS)

  • Masters in Computer Application(MCA)

Admission is based on the MHT-CET entrance exam and Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) giving students a structured and merit-based route into the college.

The college campus is located in a developing educational hub of Vasai and is being planned to offer a conducive environment for quality education. The infrastructure is expected to support both classroom learning and practical exposure, allowing students to build skills not only through theory but also through experimentation and application.

The campus aims to provide a balanced ecosystem where students can attend lectures, participate in projects, engage in discussions, and also take part in cultural and sporting activities. This holistic approach is intended to make the learning journey more enriching and memorable.

  • Smart classrooms

  • Well-equipped laboratories

  • Library and digital resources

  • Auditorium and seminar halls

  • Sports and recreational facilities

  • IT infrastructure for technical learning

TSDCEM has qualified faculty members with expertise in various engineering fields who are expected to play an important role in shaping students into capable professionals. The teaching methodology is designed to move beyond rote learning and encourage deeper understanding, curiosity, and problem-solving skills.

The institution aims to promote a strong academic culture where concepts are taught clearly, practical work is emphasized, and students are guided to connect their learning with real-world applications. Faculty members are also expected to support students in projects, internships, and overall career development.

  • Concept-based learning

  • Practical knowledge and lab work

  • Industry-oriented curriculum

  • Research and innovation

The main vision of TSDCEM is to create skilled engineers and future leaders who can contribute meaningfully to industry, society, and the nation. The institution believes that education should not only focus on academic scores but also on innovation, leadership qualities, and responsible citizenship.

TSDCEM aims to become a preferred destination for engineering and management studies in Maharashtra by offering quality education, encouraging entrepreneurship, and motivating students to become problem-solvers who can adapt to changing technological and professional landscapes.

  • Create skilled engineers and future leaders

  • Promote innovation and entrepreneurship

  • Provide quality technical education

  • Contribute to society through knowledge and technology

Students choose TSDCEM because it combines the credibility of the Thakur Educational Group with the promise of a fresh, modern learning environment. The college is being developed to offer a strong academic foundation, useful practical exposure, and the support required for future success.

With a strong emphasis on innovation, entrepreneurship, and industry-aligned learning, TSDCEM is positioned as an institution that can open doors to internships, placements, and long-term career growth. It is an ideal choice for students who want to grow both academically and professionally in a supportive setting.

  • Backed by Thakur Educational Group (trusted name)

  • Modern infrastructure and new facilities

  • Focus on innovation and startups

  • Industry-relevant curriculum

About Examination Cell

The Examination Cell is an essential unit of an educational institution responsible for managing all examination-related activities. It plans and schedules exams, prepares timetables, and ensures smooth coordination between departments. The cell handles confidential tasks such as question paper preparation, printing, and secure distribution. It also manages seating arrangements, assigns invigilators, and ensures fair conduct of examinations. After exams, the Examination Cell oversees answer sheet evaluation, result processing, and declaration of results. It also handles revaluation requests and student grievances.
